##Notes related to GDPR##

- - Not protected by GDPR: activities outside the EU; activities which fall within the scope of _Chapter 2 of Title V of the TEU_, purely personal or household activities; activities related to criminal investigation/national security.
- - Art 2.4: _"This regulation shall be without prejudice to the application of Directive 2000/21/EC, in particular of the liability rules of intermediary service providers in Article 12 to 15 of that Directive."_
